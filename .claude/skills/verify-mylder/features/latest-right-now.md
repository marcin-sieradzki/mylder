# Latest right now

The Latest now pill names the trip running furthest behind its timetable right now, anywhere on the map, among the trips whose delay is known: "Latest now [950R] +77 min at Slotspavillonen St.", its line badge, its delay in whole minutes and the stop it is at or last passed. A press opens the Latest right now panel on that trip, kept in the address as `?latest=now`, flies the map to its dot, clear of the header and the panel, and rings the dot with the spotlight until the panel closes. The panel leads with that trip's facts, Follow and Show whole line, then lists the next four latest, each a tap from its own vehicle panel, the ring and a flight to it; on a phone it is a bottom sheet. When no trip is 3 min late, when SIRI-ET's delays stop coming, or when no trip on the map shows a delay, it says so and names no trip; with the delays off there is no pill. Wider than a phone it sits in the top bar's tools wherever the bar has room for it on one row with ten characters of its stop's name, and otherwise on a row of its own at the start under the bar, as on a phone, where it always has that row. The Overview sheet and the detail panels start below the header, that row included.

## Sub-features

- `latest-standout` names the latest trip: line badge, signed whole-minute delay and "at <stop>", heard as "Biggest delay right now: <line> to <headsign>, <n> min late, between <stop> and <stop>" (or "at <stop>" where it stands at one).
- `latest-open` opens the Latest right now panel on a press, a dialog named "Latest right now", kept in the address as `?latest=now`, on a desktop beside the map below the header and on a phone from the bottom, in front of the Overview sheet.
- `latest-panel-head` leads the panel with the picked trip on a card named "The latest trip": its line badge, "to <headsign>", "<Mode> · <operator>" ("Train · DSB", "Bus · Movia"), its delay "+<n> min" and "Between <stop> and <stop>" (or "At <stop>", "Waiting at <stop>"), the Next stop fact "<stop> <timetabled> ~<expected>", the timetabled time struck through and heard "Timetabled HH:MM, expected HH:MM", its data state's dot and word ("Estimated"), and "Delay sent HH:MM, by Rejseplanen"; it keeps leading with that trip when a later snapshot names another latest.
- `latest-panel-actions` puts Follow and Show whole line on that card, acting on the picked trip as its vehicle panel's do: Follow pressed while the camera keeps to the dot, kept while the panel stays open; Show whole line raises "Showing: line <line> · Clear" and ends Follow.
- `latest-panel-list` lists the next four latest under "Next latest", the picked trip left out, each row its line badge, destination, "Next stop: <stop>" and delay, a button over the row heard "<line> to <headsign>, <n> min late, next stop <stop>"; then the source line "Of <n> trips with delays from Rejseplanen now. The Metro and light rail send none. Updated HH:MM:SS", the count grouped "1 720".
- `latest-panel-pick` taps a row: its vehicle panel opens in the Latest right now panel's place (`?trip=<key>`), the ring moves to its dot, and the camera flies there, clear of the header and the vehicle panel as from the pill.
- `latest-panel-link` opens the panel cold from `?latest=now` on the trip running latest as the page loads, rung, the camera flown to it.
- `latest-panel-calm` opens the panel from the calm pill with no trip picked and no ring: "No trip runs 3 min late or more now." over "Latest trips", the top five running late or on time; and, open as the delays go quiet or none are known, it lists nothing and says so in the pill's words.
- `latest-spotlight` flies the map to the named trip's dot on a press and rings it, and no other dot, with a red ring 24 px across: once the camera lands, the ring lies below the header (the pill's row and notices included) and clear of the open panel, between the Overview sheet and the panel on a desktop, above the panel's peek and the legend on a phone, and a hover on it shows that trip's card. The ring follows the dot as it moves, stays on the picked trip when a later snapshot names another latest (the camera doesn't move again), and goes when the panel closes, or once the pill turns calm, quiet or none. Follow, if on, ends at the press. A snapshot landing while the camera is in the air still leaves the dot in that clear room: the camera eases to it again once it rests.
- `latest-calm` reads "Latest now +2 min at most" when no trip is 3 min late, naming no trip; pressed, it opens the panel (`latest-panel-calm`).
- `latest-quiet` reads "Delays from Rejseplanen not updated since HH:MM" once SIRI-ET's feed has sent nothing for 5 min, naming no trip.
- `latest-none` reads "Latest now —" and "the Metro sends no delays" when no trip on the map shows a delay on a source that can send them, such as the `empty` recording.
- `latest-off` shows no pill with the delays off: the timetable source with SIRI-ET's feed off, the one source that can send no delay.
- `latest-phone` puts the pill on a row of its own under the top bar on a phone, never wider than the screen, "Latest now", the badge and the delay whole and at least ten characters of the stop's name in view, in English and Danish: on the pill's line where they fit, else on a second line inside the pill, a long name cut by an ellipsis.
- `latest-wide` keeps the top bar on one row whenever the pill is in it, with six chips (Bus, Metro, S-tog, Train, Light rail, Planes), in English and Danish, standing out or quiet: the pill in its tools where the bar has room for it with ten characters of its stop's name (1440 px and wider, on a long one), on its row at the start under the bar where it hasn't (800 to 1280 px), moving between the two as the window is resized; the Overview sheet's and an open panel's headings below the header, the pill's row included.
- `latest-da` says it all in Danish: "Mest forsinket nu", "Største forsinkelse lige nu: … min forsinket, mellem … og …"; the panel "Mest forsinket lige nu", "Den mest forsinkede tur", "Tog · DSB", "Mellem … og …", "Planlagt HH:MM, forventet HH:MM", "Forsinkelse sendt HH:MM af Rejseplanen", "Næste mest forsinkede", "Næste stop: …", "Følg", "Vis hele linjen", "Ud af … ture med forsinkelser fra Rejseplanen nu. Metroen og letbanen sender ingen. Opdateret …".

## How to get to it (user POV)

- On a launch with SIRI-ET's delays (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> updates:siri-et`), open `/en`; the pill is in the top bar once the timetable has loaded.
- Press the pill.
- On a launch on the quiet moment (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> updates:siri-et/quiet`), open `/en` once the feed has gone quiet.
- On the `weekday` recording, open `/en`: Rejseplanen's own delays there name a trip too.
- On a launch on the `empty` recording (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> empty`), open `/en`: "Latest now —".
- On a launch on the timetable source (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> timetable`), open `/en`: no pill.
- Open `/da` for the Danish words; add `--phone` for the phone's layout.

## Driving it with drive.ts

Preconditions:

- A launch with `updates:siri-et` as the third argument, a minute after launch so the table has filled (timings in [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md), Launch, "SIRI-ET's delays"); `doctor.sh` healthy.
- The trip the pill must name is read from `await (await page.request.get('/api/snapshot')).json()` at drive time: of its `trips` with no `cancelled`, a `delay` and a `state` of `live`, `ageing` or `estimated`, the one with the largest `delay`, a tie going to the smaller `key`. Its line is `line`, its headsign `headsign`, its delay `Math.round(delay / 60)` minutes. A snapshot lands each minute, so read it and check the pill inside `expect(...).toPass()`.

- **Standout.** `page.getByRole('banner').getByRole('button', { name: /^Biggest delay right now: / })` is visible; its name starts `Biggest delay right now: <line> to <headsign>, <n> min late, ` and goes on `between <stop> and <stop>` or `at <stop>`; its text holds `Latest now`, `<line>`, `+<n> min` and `at <stop>`, that stop the first of the two in its name, or its only one. `n` is 3 or more; when it isn't, the minute is calm (below).
- **Open.** Click the pill; the address has `?latest=now` and no `trip=`, and `page.getByRole('dialog', { name: 'Latest right now' })` is visible; on a desktop its box starts below `getByRole('banner')`'s bottom, on a phone it rises from the screen's foot.
- **Panel head.** In that dialog, `getByRole('region', { name: 'The latest trip' })` holds the line's badge, `getByRole('heading', { name: `to ${headsign}` })`, `<Mode> · <operator>`, `.pn-Delay` reading `+<n> min`, `Between <stop> and <stop>` or `At <stop>`, and `.pn-VehicleDotLabel` with the trip's state word (`estimated` → `Estimated`). Its Next stop value (`getByText('Next stop', { selector: 'dt' })`'s next sibling) starts with the stop named by `trips[i].next[0]` and shows two times, the first inside `<s>`, struck: the timetabled time, `next[0].time - delay * 1000`, then `~` and `next[0].time` on Copenhagen's clock, both `HH:MM`. `Delay sent HH:MM, by Rejseplanen` gives the trip's `updated` on Copenhagen's clock. Nothing in the card spills: `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` on the dialog's body and on the card, at 360 px in Danish too.
- **Panel actions.** On the card, `Follow` turns `aria-pressed="true"`; drag the map and it goes off, as Follow does; `Show whole line` raises the highlight chip `Showing: line <line> · Clear`.
- **Panel list.** `getByRole('region', { name: 'Next latest' }).getByRole('button')` counts 4: the ranking's second to fifth (as in the precondition, the next four by `delay`), each named `<line> to <headsign>, <n> min late, next stop <stop>`. The dialog's `.pn-SourceLine` reads `Of <count> trips with delays from Rejseplanen now. The Metro and light rail send none. Updated HH:MM:SS`, `<count>` the trips with no `cancelled`, a `delay` and a delay-known state, grouped by a space in thousands, the time `updates.lastMessage`'s.
- **Panel pick.** Click the second row's button: the address has `?trip=<its key>` and no `latest=`, `getByRole('dialog', { name: new RegExp(`to ${headsign}`) })` is visible, `data-spotlight` turns to its key, and once the camera rests the ring is clear of the header and that panel as in Spotlight below; a hover on the ring shows that trip's card.
- **Panel link.** Open `/en?latest=now` cold (a new page): the dialog shows with the card on the trip the precondition names, `data-spotlight` is its key, and the ring comes to rest clear as in Spotlight.
- **Panel calm.** On a launch on the `crowded` recording (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> crowded`: Rejseplanen's own delays, every known one 0, so none 3 min late) the pill reads `Latest now 0 min at most`, a button; click it: `?latest=now`, the dialog holds `No trip runs 3 min late or more now.` (or `Every trip with a delay runs early now.` where every one runs early), `getByRole('region', { name: 'Latest trips' })` with up to five rows, the trips running late or on time, latest first (on `crowded`, its trains on time, each named `<line> to <headsign>, on time, next stop <stop>` and showing `On time`), no region named `The latest trip`, and the map has no `data-spotlight`.
- **Spotlight.** At each of 412 px (`--phone`), 1280 and 1440 px (`page.setViewportSize({ width, height: 800 })`), with the Overview sheet open, click the pill. The map's element `[data-vehicles]` gets `data-spotlight="<key>"`, the trip the panel's card names, the one ringed trip, and `data-spotlight-at="<x> <y>"`, the ring's centre in px from the page's top left, written each frame while it is drawn. Once the camera lands (poll inside `expect(...).toPass()`, a few seconds), with the ring's outer radius 12 px: `y - 12` is below the bottom of `getByRole('banner')`; on a desktop `x - 12` is right of the `Overview` dialog's box and `x + 12` left of the open panel's (`Latest right now`, or the trip's after a row's tap); on a phone `y + 12` is above that panel's top and above `getByRole('list', { name: 'Data states' })`'s top. `page.mouse.move(x, y)` (after a move off it) shows `getByRole('tooltip')` with `<line> to <headsign>`. Press again just as a minute's snapshot lands (the Updated time turns over, or the page's `/api/snapshot` request goes out: `page.waitForRequest('**/api/snapshot')` then click) and the same holds once the camera rests, after a short second ease where the snapshot moved the dot. Close the panel with its `Close` button: `data-spotlight` and `data-spotlight-at` go. To see the ring stay, wait for the next minute's snapshot with the panel open: `data-spotlight` keeps the key whatever the pill names, and the map doesn't move; the vehicle may run off the view, as the camera no longer follows it.
- **Calm.** `updates:siri-et` promises no calm minute; the `crowded` recording is one (Panel calm above): when `/api/snapshot`'s largest known delay rounds under 3 min, `page.getByRole('banner').getByRole('button', { name: /^Latest now [+]\d+ min at most$/ })` shows the largest, and no button named `/^Biggest delay/` is there.
- **None.** On a launch on the `empty` recording (`launch.sh <run-id> <port> empty`: Rejseplanen's answers, every trip Timetable only, none with a delay), the banner's `getByText(/^Latest now —/)` is visible and holds `the Metro sends no delays`, with no button named `/^Biggest delay/`; on `/da`, `getByText(/^Mest forsinket nu —/)` holds `Metroen sender ingen forsinkelser`.
- **Quiet.** On a launch with `updates:siri-et/quiet`, once `/api/snapshot`'s `updates` reads `"fresh":false` (about 6 minutes in), the banner's `getByText('Delays from Rejseplanen not updated since 09:56')` is visible and no button named `/^Biggest delay/` is there; on `/da`, `Forsinkelser fra Rejseplanen ikke opdateret siden 09:56`. Before then the pill names a trip as in Standout.
- **Off.** On a launch with `timetable` as the third argument, `page.getByRole('banner').getByText(/Latest now|Delays from Rejseplanen/)` has a count of 0.
- **Danish.** On `/da` the button's name starts `Største forsinkelse lige nu: <line> mod <headsign>, <n> min forsinket, ` and its text holds `Mest forsinket nu` and `ved <stop>`. Pressed, the dialog is `Mest forsinket lige nu`, its card `Den mest forsinkede tur` with `mod <headsign>`, `Planlagt HH:MM, forventet HH:MM` heard in its Næste stop, `Forsinkelse sendt HH:MM af Rejseplanen`, `Følg` and `Vis hele linjen`; the list `Næste mest forsinkede`, rows named `<line> mod <headsign>, <n> min forsinket, næste stop <stop>`. At 360 px the longest words (`Næste mest forsinkede`, a long stop in brackets) wrap inside the panel, never past it.
- **Phone.** With `--phone` (and again at 360 px, `page.setViewportSize({ width: 360, height: 800 })`), on `/en` and `/da`, the pill's `boundingBox()` starts at or below the bottom of the About button's, lies inside the viewport and inside its row's glass (`pill.locator('xpath=..')`), and `pill.evaluate((e) => e.scrollWidth <= e.clientWidth)` is true; `pill.getByText('Latest now')` (`Mest forsinket nu`), the badge and `pill.locator('.pn-Delay')` each lie inside the pill's box, and at least ten characters of the stop's name show before any ellipsis. Where ten won't fit beside the delay, as at 360 px in Danish on a long name, the `[data-latest-where]` span starts below "Latest now", on a second line; at 412 px in English on the same name it stays on the first. A long stop name, such as one with a street in brackets, ends in an ellipsis: its `<span>`'s `scrollWidth` exceeds its `clientWidth`, and the button's name still holds the whole name. The Overview sheet's heading starts below `getByRole('banner')`'s bottom.
- **Wide.** At `page.setViewportSize({ width, height: 800 })` for each of 800, 900, 1000, 1100, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px on `updates:siri-et`, whose launch has the planes, the `Filters` group holds six buttons. Wherever the pill is, `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` holds on it, `Latest now`, the badge and the `.pn-Delay` lie inside its box, and at least ten characters of its stop's name show before any ellipsis. Where it is in the bar (`pill.evaluate((e) => e.closest('.pn-TopBar') !== null)`), the brand link `Mylder`'s middle is level with the About button's (one row) and the `.pn-TopBar` is no taller than 58 px. The Overview sheet's `Overview` heading, and after a press on the pill the `Latest right now` dialog's heading, start at or below the bottom of `getByRole('banner')`, the pill's row included. At 900 px the pill is on its row, its glass's left edge level with the bar's; at 1440 px on a long stop name it is in the bar, cut by an ellipsis; resizing from 1440 to 1000 px moves it onto its row, and back to 1440 px into the bar again. Again on `/da`, and on `updates:siri-et/quiet` once it is quiet. Which place it takes depends on the minute's stop name and the chips' counts.

## Gotchas

- The pill waits for the timetable to load: give it the default timeout before asserting it is absent, and assert absence only after the Updated line and the mode chips show.
- The ranking is the whole map's, whatever the mode filters show: hiding a mode never changes the trip the pill names.
- On the `weekday` recording the delays are Rejseplanen's own, with SIRI-ET's off, so the pill names a trip there too (the S-tog A at 07:07, +4 min, in its first minute), and never says the feed is quiet.
- In the first seconds after launch, before the feed's first message, the pill reads "Delays from Rejseplanen not received yet": wait a minute.
- The spotlight's attributes are on the map's own element, beside `data-vehicles`; the ring has no ARIA of its own: the open panel names the trip.
- The Latest right now panel reads the snapshot alone, so it asks no route: its card shows at once, and its times are the snapshot's next call's, which the trip's vehicle panel, from the trip route, gives the same.
- A press made while calm picks no trip, and the panel never picks one later in that opening, even once a trip runs 3 min late: close it and press the pill again.
- A snapshot can land between reading `/api/snapshot` and reading the pill: compare them inside `toPass()`.
